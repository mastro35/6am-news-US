## Front Page

### Trump says Iran war is “effectively over” and further U.S. strikes are unlikely
President Donald Trump said the conflict with Iran was “effectively over” and that additional U.S. military action was highly unlikely for now, while warning that U.S. forces remained ready if circumstances changed. His comments followed a meeting with Qatar’s prime minister and helped ease fears of further escalation. [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/nov/09/trump-iran-war) - [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cq5yny4nl2d3o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

### U.S., Russia and Ukraine begin first substantive peace talks in months
Delegations from Washington, Moscow and Kyiv began trilateral talks in Abu Dhabi, the first substantive negotiations between Russia and Ukraine since February. U.S. officials said they would pursue a lasting end to the war, with discussions including a proposed long-term security framework. European officials have pressed for a role in the negotiations, while Ukraine’s sovereignty and security guarantees remain central issues. [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/10/us-officials-russia-ukraine-war-abu-dhabi-talks) - [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqpvgvrpjvv8o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

### Nvidia scandal puts CEO Jensen Huang and the company’s governance under scrutiny
President Trump said the FBI is investigating Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang’s relationship with a woman alleged to have ties to Chinese intelligence; Trump also said he believed Huang did not know of the allegation. Nvidia and Huang are facing a shareholder lawsuit over disclosures, while governance experts are questioning the board’s oversight and the company’s lack of a named successor. ### Nearly 30 runners hospitalized after Santa Barbara half-marathon

## Politics

### California voters reject proposed one-time billionaire wealth tax
California voters defeated Proposition 40, which would have imposed a one-time 5% tax on billionaires’ wealth to help fund health care. The expensive campaign became a test of whether a state could tax wealth directly, with opponents arguing the measure could harm the economy and supporters presenting it as a response to anticipated health care cuts. [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/09/us/california-billionaire-tax-prop-40.html) - [The Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/politics/elections/california-voters-reject-unprecedented-tax-on-billionaires-51707619?mod=rss_socialpoliticsfeed)

### Republican Thomas Massie moves to force impeachment vote on Defense Secretary Hegseth
Rep. Thomas Massie of Kentucky has set in motion a House vote on impeaching Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth, citing alleged unlawful orders connected to the Iran war. The effort is expected to face long odds, but could force lawmakers to take a public position before Democrats gain more influence in Congress. [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/09/us/politics/hegseth-impeachment-massie.html) - [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/9/us-congressman-files-resolution-to-impeach-defence-secretary-pete-hegseth?traffic_source=rss)

### Supreme Court clears way for border wall construction in Big Bend
The Supreme Court allowed the Trump administration to resume border-wall construction in Texas’s Big Bend region, including areas of Big Bend National Park. The unsigned ruling paused a lower-court order that had halted the project amid concerns about environmental and recreational impacts. [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/nov/09/us-supreme-court-border-wall-texas-big-bend) - [The Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/us-news/law/trump-border-wall-big-bend-scotus-2ae1419e?mod=rss_usnews)

### Senate advances bipartisan bill to speed up infrastructure permits
The Senate voted 73-25 to take up legislation that would shorten permitting timelines for energy, data-center and other infrastructure projects. Supporters say the measure could help address growing electricity demand and delays in connecting new energy sources to the grid. [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/nov/09/senate-advances-bill-energy-projects)
