### Dollar falls as investors weigh political gridlock and AI regulation
The dollar hit a one-month low after Democrats’ victories, as investors assessed the possibility of tougher regulation, including for artificial intelligence, and a more constrained White House. Treasury yields also declined, while gold gained support from the weaker dollar and demand for safer assets.
