## Front Page

### Trump pauses planned Iran strikes, citing prospects for a rapid deal

President Trump said he was canceling planned attacks on Iran after what he described as progress toward the “perimeters of a deal” with Tehran. The decision followed requests from Iran and other Middle Eastern countries, while regional tensions remained high after threats of retaliation and reports of drone attacks and attacks on ships.  
[The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/02/world/middleeast/trump-iran-cancels-attack-deal.html) - [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/aug/02/trump-climbs-down-on-iran-strikes-after-saudi-alarm-and-threats-from-tehran)

### Three killed in shooting at Idaho In-N-Out restaurant

Three people were killed and seven others were injured, some critically, in a shooting at an In-N-Out Burger restaurant in Twin Falls, Idaho. Police found the suspected gunman dead near the restaurant, according to a city spokesperson.  
[Associated Press via The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/aug/02/shooting-at-in-n-out-burger-restaurant-in-idaho-leaves-multiple-people-dead-police-say) - [The Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/us-news/three-dead-at-least-five-injured-in-idaho-shooting-at-in-n-out-restaurant-63390e21)

### Ceuta migrant surge reshapes Europe’s political debate

Most of the migrants who entered Spain’s North African enclave of Ceuta have returned to Morocco, while a small group remains on the seafront. The episode has prompted an emergency European Union meeting and intensified debate over migration, with the New York Times reporting that Europe’s far right is setting the tone of the response.  
[The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/02/world/europe/ceuta-spain-morocco-migrant-holdouts-beach.html) - [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cz7d17r455go?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## National News

### Western heat wave heightens wildfire danger

A heat wave is raising the risk of dangerous wildfire conditions in Oregon and Washington, where low humidity and strong winds have put officials on alert. Forecast temperatures across the Southwest could reach 110 to 120 degrees Fahrenheit, while the Pacific Northwest is expected to see highs in the 90s.  
[The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/aug/01/heatwave-us-west-hazardous-fire-conditions) - [Los Angeles Times](https://www.latimes.com/california/story/2026-08-01/western-us-heatwave-record-temps-wildfire-risk)

### Cyberattacks on water systems reported across at least seven states

Michigan and Minnesota are among at least seven states dealing with cyberattacks aimed at disrupting water systems, according to the New York Times. The FBI is investigating, and no culprit has been identified in the attacks.  
[The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/01/us/politics/iran-cyberattack-water-systems.html) - [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/1/michigan-joins-minnesota-in-reporting-cyber-attacks-with-fbi-investigating?traffic_source=rss)

### Ebola outbreak in Congo described as the country’s worst ever

The World Health Organization says the current Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of Congo is the country’s worst on record. The strain has killed 1,587 people, and there is no approved vaccine or treatment, according to the BBC.

### Moscow restaurant bombing kills three and injures at least 21

A homemade bomb carried by a woman exploded near a restaurant in central Moscow, killing three people and injuring at least 21, authorities said. The blast occurred near Kudrinskaya Square, one of the city’s Stalin-era skyscrapers.
