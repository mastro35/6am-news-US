## National News

### Investigators recover flight recorders from Miami cargo-plane crash

Investigators recovered the flight recorders from an Amazon Boeing 767-300 that overshot the runway at Miami International Airport. Five people were killed and five others were seriously injured. The National Transportation Safety Board is examining the aircraft’s speed and airport runway safeguards after the plane traveled 1,300 feet beyond the runway and struck vehicles, including a van carrying seven people.
