### Study finds widespread physical and sexual violence among U.S. adults

A national study led by researchers at Tulane University’s Newcomb Institute and the University of California, San Diego, found that more than half of U.S. adults have experienced physical violence and nearly half have experienced sexual violence. Researchers also examined links with intimate-partner violence, behavioral health, economic hardship, neighborhood safety and substance use.

[The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/sep/16/physical-sexual-violence-study-us)
