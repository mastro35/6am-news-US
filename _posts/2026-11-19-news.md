## Front Page

### Trump, Xi and Putin hold an unprecedented three-way summit in China
President Donald Trump met with Chinese leader Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin in Shenzhen, marking the first summit of its kind. Trump called the gathering historic and said he planned to raise the war in Ukraine and nuclear arms reduction. The meeting also underscored a shift in global diplomacy, as U.S. allies watch how Washington’s engagement with Beijing and Moscow may affect existing partnerships. ### World court finds genocidal intent in Myanmar’s treatment of Rohingya
The International Court of Justice issued a landmark ruling in a case brought by The Gambia, finding genocidal intent in Myanmar’s campaign against the Rohingya Muslim minority. The case stems from the military crackdown that drove hundreds of thousands of Rohingya from the country in 2017. The ruling is a major development in international efforts to establish accountability for the atrocities. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/19/un-top-court-finds-myanmar-killed-10000-rohingya-as-it-rules-on-genocide?traffic_source=rss) - [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c5m2l90ez144o) - [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/19/world/asia/myanmar-rohingya-village-refugee-genocide.html)
