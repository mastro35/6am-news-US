## Front Page

### Russia reports massive Ukrainian drone attack as parliamentary voting ends

Russian authorities said hundreds of Ukrainian drones targeted Moscow and surrounding areas on the final day of parliamentary elections. At least two people were killed, an oil refinery was damaged and flights were suspended at two airports. Moscow’s mayor said 450 drones aimed at the capital were intercepted, as part of a broader total of 1,600 drones downed since Saturday. Ukraine said it used a newly developed ballistic missile in the attacks.
