### ICE agent shoots and wounds Venezuelan man in Austin

An ICE officer shot Wilber Rafael Garcés Pérez, a 28-year-old Venezuelan man, during a traffic stop in Austin, Texas, officials said. Pérez was hospitalized in serious but stable condition after being shot once in the torso. Demonstrators gathered at the scene, demanding transparency as the Trump administration intensifies its immigration enforcement campaign.
