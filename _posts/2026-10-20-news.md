## Front Page

### Federal voter-fraud charges intensify election dispute in California
The Justice Department has charged 28 green-card holders, alleging they fraudulently registered to vote and cast illegal ballots; 11 were reported in custody. The arrests come amid a broader federal challenge to California’s election procedures. State officials and voting-rights advocates say the administration’s claims of widespread noncitizen voting are unsupported, while the defendants’ cases remain allegations, not convictions. [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/oct/20/first-thing-federal-efforts-over-alleged-noncitizen-voting-escalate-with-california-arrests) - [Los Angeles Times](https://www.latimes.com/california/story/2026-10-19/california-voter-fraud-charges-defendants-react)

### Oil tops $100 a barrel as Middle East conflict threatens supplies
Crude futures climbed above $100 a barrel, with Brent trading above $110, as attacks on ships in the Strait of Hormuz and Houthi strikes on Saudi Arabia heightened concerns about energy flows. The price surge raises the risk of added fuel and transportation costs for U.S. consumers and businesses. [The Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/business/energy-oil/oil-prices-up-as-traders-weigh-middle-east-supply-risks-560c31ca?mod=pls_whats_news_us_business_f)

## Crime & Local News

### Mississippi rules out criminal charges in fatal police shooting of toddler
Mississippi Attorney General Lynn Fitch said the state will not bring criminal charges in the police shooting of one-year-old Kohen Wiley. Officers had surrounded a car while responding to a report of stolen diapers; authorities said the vehicle was driven toward officers. The decision follows months of scrutiny over the shooting. [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/20/us/mississippi-police-shooting-toddler-kohen-wiley.html)

### Las Vegas visitor convicted after taking casino flamingo
A Canadian tourist was found guilty of animal cruelty after taking a flamingo, named Peachy, from an enclosure at the Flamingo hotel and casino. He testified that he had been drinking heavily and did not intend to harm the bird. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cq398rjewd1jo)

### Oregon town seeks accountability after school shooting
Residents of Bend, Oregon, are looking for answers after a 16-year-old attacker died in a shooting at Silver Rail Elementary. A legal barrier makes it difficult to hold officials accountable, according to the report, leaving families and community members with limited options to pursue responsibility. [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/19/us/bend-school-shooting-accountability.html)

## Politics

### Kansas Senate race puts a historically safe Republican seat in play
Democrat Adam Hamilton is mounting an unexpectedly strong challenge to Republican Sen. Roger Marshall, with some polls showing a close contest. Kansas has not elected a Democrat to the Senate since 1932. President Trump campaigned for Marshall in the state, underscoring the race’s potential significance for Republicans ahead of the midterms. [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/oct/20/kansas-senate-race-democrat-adam-hamilton) - [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/oct/20/trump-kansas-rally-us-midterm-election-possibility-state-democratic-win)

### Republicans shift $50 million to support midterm candidates
The Republican National Committee is moving $50 million to help congressional candidates, taking advantage of its substantial cash reserves and a recent Supreme Court campaign-finance ruling. The transfer signals an effort to bolster the party’s candidates as both parties prepare for closely contested midterm races. [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/20/us/politics/republicans-midterm-election-cash-transfer.html)

### Trump’s approval rating hits a historic low ahead of midterms
An analysis by Al Jazeera says President Trump’s approval rating has fallen to a historic low, even as presidents commonly face declining support before midterm elections. The report examines what the numbers could mean for the November vote, with voters weighing the economy and the administration’s policies. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/20/trumps-approval-ratings-plummet-what-that-means-for-the-us-midterms)

## Economy & Finance

### Trump announces $8 billion in aid for farmers facing higher fuel costs
The administration plans to provide $8 billion in economic assistance to farmers affected by high diesel prices through a program called Harvest Fuel Relief, according to the Agriculture Department. The proposed support comes as rising energy costs add pressure to farm operations and input expenses. [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/20/business/trump-farmer-bailout.html)

### GM plans $20 billion in stock buybacks
General Motors plans to spend $20 billion buying back its shares, saying executives believe the stock is undervalued even as auto sales cool. Buybacks can return cash to investors and support a company’s share price, but the announcement comes amid questions about the outlook for the auto market. [The Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/business/autos/general-motors-plans-20-billion-in-buybacks-to-shore-up-share-price-a857e6fe?mod=pls_whats_news_us_business_f)

### RTX raises its outlook again as defense and aerospace demand grows
RTX lifted its forecast for the third time this year after reporting more than $81 billion in new commercial and defense awards in the latest quarter. The company’s backlog reached a record $340 billion, highlighting sustained demand for its aerospace and defense products. [The Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/business/earnings/rtx-lifts-forecast-for-third-time-this-year-on-aerospace-defense-demand-e40e03e0?mod=pls_whats_news_us_business_f)

### Halliburton profit jumps as revenue climbs
The Houston-based oil-services company reported third-quarter profit of $417 million, or 50 cents a share, up from $18 million, or 2 cents a share, a year earlier. The results reflect a sharp improvement in earnings as energy markets face heightened supply concerns. [The Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/business/earnings/halliburton-logs-sharply-higher-profit-as-revenue-climbs-93093376?mod=pls_whats_news_us_business_f)
