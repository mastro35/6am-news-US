### U.S. and Iran Exchange Strikes as Tensions Rise in the Strait of Hormuz

U.S. forces struck two Iranian rocket launchers on Larak Island, the first publicly disclosed U.S. attack on Iran in nearly a month. ## National News

### Grand Canyon Flash Flood Leaves One Dead and About 15 Missing

A flash flood swept through Bright Angel Canyon and the Phantom Ranch area of Grand Canyon National Park on Saturday. One person died and about 15 people were missing or unaccounted for, according to the National Park Service. More than 60 people were evacuated, while footbridges, a campground and cabins were damaged.

[The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/30/us/grand-canyon-flash-flood-people-missing.html) - [Los Angeles Times](https://www.latimes.com/environment/story/2026-08-30/grand-canyon-flash-flood-leaves-more-than-20-missing)

### USDA Reportedly Shelves Cyclospora Research Projects

The U.S. Department of Agriculture is reportedly closing two research projects studying cyclospora, a foodborne parasite that has sickened tens of thousands of Americans this summer. The projects were defunded by Congress earlier in the year, while a third project is expected to move from Maryland to Iowa as part of a broader USDA reorganization.

[The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/aug/30/usda-cyclospora-research-projects-shelved)
