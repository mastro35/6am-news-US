## Front Page

### Democrats’ midterm sweep reshapes the balance of power
Democrats’ decisive midterm gains extended beyond Congress, with the party also winning hundreds of state legislative seats. The results mark a sharp reversal for Republicans and could give Democrats new power over policy in states including Texas, Arizona and Wisconsin. [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/nov/05/democrats-state-legislatures-midterm-elections) - [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/04/us/politics/trump-midterms-losses-republicans.html)

### Trump’s Iran war faces new scrutiny after Republican losses
President Donald Trump’s weakened position after the midterms could complicate his handling of the war in Iran. Democrats are preparing to use their control of Congress to increase oversight and contest war-related spending, while Vice President JD Vance has publicly blamed the conflict for the GOP’s poor showing. [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/05/us/politics/trump-iran-war-midterm-elections.html) - [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/5/congress-defeat-how-trumps-allies-are-distancing-themselves-from-iran-war)

## Politics

### Vance breaks with Trump on Iran war as Republicans reckon with defeat
Vance called the Iran war an “obvious” factor in the Republicans’ midterm losses, offering a striking public critique of the administration’s policy. Trump has also acknowledged the conflict may have hurt his party, even as he continues to defend the military campaign. [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/04/us/politics/vance-iran-war-midterms-trump.html) - [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/crz98zqkz7z0o)

### Democrats make historic gains in state legislatures
Democrats won hundreds of state legislative seats, with results pointing to major shifts in several states. The party is poised to gain unified control in Arizona and Pennsylvania and regain governing majorities in Michigan and Minnesota; in Texas, Democrats are set to take control of the state House for the first time since 2002. [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/nov/05/democrats-state-legislatures-midterm-elections)

### Trump accepts midterm results after months of election warnings
Trump said he was willing to accept the election outcome and work with Democrats, despite an administration campaign that had raised doubts about the vote and put pressure on election officials. The Guardian reports that the election system held up and the anticipated challenges to the results did not materialize. [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/nov/05/trump-election-interference-midterm-results)

## Economy & Finance

### Treasury yields fall as investors anticipate a political check on spending
U.S. Treasury yields dropped to eight-week lows after Democrats’ gains raised expectations of fiscal restraint and political gridlock. The dollar also remained under pressure, while gold climbed as lower yields boosted its appeal. [The Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/finance/investing/u-s-treasury-yields-fall-to-eight-week-lows-as-markets-digest-midterm-results-7effce3e)

### Oil stays below $100 as traders weigh Iran diplomacy and supply
Oil prices remained below $100 a barrel as traders assessed the prospects for U.S.-Iran diplomacy and the return of disrupted Middle Eastern supplies. The election result has raised hopes among some investors that political pressure could push the conflict toward an end, though uncertainty remains. [The Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/business/energy-oil/oil-prices-stay-below-100-as-traders-weigh-iran-diplomacy-supply-outlook-4e8f12e5)

### AI stocks face pressure as investors reassess the sector
Artificial-intelligence shares continued to slide as investors reconsidered their positions after the U.S. election and weighed signs of public resistance to large data center projects. Qualcomm’s disappointing earnings added to the pressure on technology stocks. [The Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/finance/stocks/ai-selloff-extends-treasurys-gain-as-blue-wave-reverberates-2d60fa21)

## World News

### Hong Kong activist Joshua Wong sentenced to 10 years in prison
Joshua Wong, a prominent figure in Hong Kong’s pro-democracy movement, was sentenced to 10 years after pleading guilty to “foreign collusion” under the territory’s national security law. Rights advocates say the law has been used to suppress political opposition. [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/04/world/asia/joshua-wong-hong-kong-sentenced.html) - [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/05/hong-kong-joshua-wong-sentenced-national-security-case)

### France braces for nationwide strikes and demonstrations
Unions called for a national day of action over the cost of living, with more than 220 demonstrations expected and workers across public services, transport and education preparing to join. The protests add to weeks of unrest and pressure on President Emmanuel Macron’s government. [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/05/world/europe/france-protests-general-strike.html) - [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/05/protesters-france-march-strikes-education-cost-of-living)

### Russia and Ukraine trade strikes on logistics and energy sites
Russia said it struck a Ukrainian postal hub and a ship, while Ukraine launched attacks on the Russian city of Ryazan. Injuries were reported on both sides amid overnight drone strikes targeting logistics and energy infrastructure. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/5/russia-says-it-hit-ukrainian-postal-hub-and-ship-as-ukraine-strikes-ryazan)

### Israeli leaders begin talks on forming a governing coalition
President Isaac Herzog began consultations with party leaders over who should get the first opportunity to form a government after Israel’s election. The process comes amid political uncertainty and efforts to assemble a workable coalition. [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/05/world/middleeast/israel-herzog-prime-minister.html)

### Voters reject data center projects in a warning to the AI industry
Voters approved nearly every data center moratorium put before them in Tuesday’s elections, across dozens of local jurisdictions in 10 states. The results signal growing opposition to facilities that power AI and other online services, raising questions about the pace and location of future expansion. [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/nov/04/datacenters-midterm-elections) - [The Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/the-election-day-revolt-that-threatens-americas-ai-boom-03a53b52)

### Ballot measures strengthen reproductive-rights protections in several states
Voters in Nevada, Virginia, Missouri and Idaho approved or strengthened measures to protect reproductive freedom. In Nevada, voters approved a constitutional amendment protecting abortion access through the second trimester, following a second referendum on the measure. [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/society/2026/nov/05/abortion-ballot-measures-pass)

### Data center backlash could benefit the biggest technology companies
Amazon, Microsoft and other large firms may gain from a backlash that makes it harder for smaller data center developers to build new facilities. The Wall Street Journal reports that resistance to projects could reshape competition even as it threatens to slow the expansion of AI infrastructure. [The Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/how-the-data-center-backlash-could-play-into-the-hands-of-big-tech-a37dfb29)
