### Deadly Floods Leave Hundreds Missing on Nepal-Tibet Border

At least 157 people were killed after flash floods swept through Himalayan border areas in Nepal and Tibet, according to police. Hundreds of people, including foreign tourists, remain missing. ### FDA Approves Daraxonrasib for Pancreatic Cancer

The Food and Drug Administration approved daraxonrasib, a new pill for the most common form of pancreatic cancer. In a key clinical trial, patients who received the drug lived twice as long as those who received chemotherapy. The treatment blocks a mutated protein that fuels tumor growth in more than 90% of pancreatic cancer cases.

[The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/26/science/pancreatic-cancer-daraxonrasib-approval.html) - [The Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/health/2026/08/26/fda-approves-landmark-pancreatic-cancer-drug-daraxonrasib/)
