## Front Page

### Microsoft outage disrupts services worldwide
A global Microsoft outage disrupted Outlook, Teams, Copilot and other Microsoft 365 services across multiple countries. Microsoft said the problem was not caused by a cybersecurity incident, but its recovery efforts were slower than expected; the company later reported early signs of stabilization. The disruption affected essential services, including hospitals, schools and police in the United Kingdom, as well as government agencies and schools in Australia. [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/nov/13/microsoft-outage-outlook-teams-word-excel-reports) - [BBC News](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cwvg238zrnm5o)

### Nvidia founder Jensen Huang resigns amid scandal and scrutiny
Jensen Huang’s departure as Nvidia CEO has prompted questions about the company’s leadership and U.S. controls on advanced chip exports to China. Reports have tied the controversy to Huang’s relationship with Alice Huang, who has been accused of being a Chinese spy; the Justice Department has said it has not concluded that she was one. The Senate Banking Committee has called on Huang and Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick to testify as scrutiny grows over the government’s handling of chip sales and national-security concerns. [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/12/us/politics/lutnick-nvidia-commerce-china-chips.html) - [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/nov/13/nvidia-scandal-sparks-us-national-security-concerns-china) - [BBC News](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cmq5vd1wdgz6o)

### Oil market may return to surplus as Gulf exports recover, IEA says
