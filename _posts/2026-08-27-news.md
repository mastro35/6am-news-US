### Nepal-Tibet Floods Leave Hundreds Dead and Nearly 1,500 Missing

A catastrophic flash flood swept through the Nepal-Tibet border region, destroying villages, roads and bridges. Authorities have reported hundreds of deaths and nearly 1,500 people missing, many of them tourists from countries including the United States, the United Kingdom, India, Australia and Canada. Preliminary investigations indicate that a collapsed glacier may have triggered the disaster, while damaged infrastructure is complicating rescue and relief operations.
