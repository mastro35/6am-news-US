### Trump Announces Board of Peace Has Reached Gaza Disarmament Agreement

President Donald Trump has announced that his newly established international organization, the Board of Peace, has secured a landmark agreement for the "complete disarmament of Hamas" in Gaza. The proposed roadmap outlines a multiphase process in which Hamas will surrender its weapons and hand over administration of the enclave to an apolitical committee, enabling a gradual withdrawal of Israeli military forces. While Board of Peace representatives have hailed the deal as a critical milestone, subsequent briefings from both Israeli and Hamas officials reveal unresolved disputes regarding its implementation, prompting world leaders to approach the announcement with caution.

### Iran Claims Drone Strikes Target U.S. Military Assets in Kuwait

The Iranian military and Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps have claimed responsibility for drone strikes targeting U.S. military assets stationed at an air base in Kuwait. ## World News

### Spain Deploys Military After Massive Migrant Crossing in Ceuta

Spain has deployed military forces and extra police units to Ceuta, its North African enclave bordering Morocco, after an estimated 49,000 migrants crossed the border by land and sea within a 24-hour period. Authorities confirmed that at least 18 people drowned or died in a crush during the chaotic crossing, prompting Spanish Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez to travel directly to the territory. In response to the migration crisis, the Italian government has publicly demanded that Spain's membership in the open-border European Schengen zone be suspended.

### Iconic AC Milan and Italian Soccer Star Franco Baresi Dies at 66

Italian soccer legend Franco Baresi, celebrated as one of the finest defenders of his generation, has died at the age of 66. Baresi, who recently underwent surgery to treat a pulmonary nodule, spent his entire career at AC Milan and represented the Italian national team during a highly successful career spanning the 1980s and 1990s. AC Milan released a statement expressing deep mourning for the loss, noting that Baresi's legacy of integrity and his iconic No. 6 jersey are permanently woven into the identity of the club.
