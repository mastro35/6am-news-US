## Front Page

### Kenya Confirms First Ebola Case and Death

Kenya has recorded its first confirmed Ebola case. The patient, a Kenyan citizen who had been living in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, arrived in Nairobi on Saturday and later died. Health officials said 28 contacts, including family members and health workers, have been identified. The BBC reported that more than 4,000 people have died from Ebola in the Democratic Republic of the Congo this year.

[Reuters via The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/oct/06/kenya-first-ebola-case-death-drc) - [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqzrd7v1j163o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

### Trump Remarks About Possible Attacks on Los Angeles and San Diego Draw Condemnation

President Donald Trump suggested at a Nebraska rally that Iran could “take out” Los Angeles or San Diego while discussing the war with Iran and the cost of keeping the country safe. California Gov. [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/06/world/asia/trump-los-angeles-san-diego-iran.html) - [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/oct/06/trump-suggests-let-iran-take-out-los-angeles-san-diego)

### U.S. Military Withdraws Bombers From British Base After Iranian Drone Threat

Twelve U.S. B-1 bombers were withdrawn from Britain’s RAF Fairford because of the risk of a drone attack ordered by Iran’s Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps, according to U.S. and British officials cited by The New York Times. The Pentagon confirmed that the aircraft had returned to their home stations in the United States.

## National News

### New York Declares Measles Emergency as Cases Reach 108

New York Gov. Kathy Hochul declared a measles emergency as the state reported 108 cases this year, the highest number since 2019. The declaration comes amid a national resurgence of the highly contagious disease and several outbreaks linked to declining vaccination rates.

[The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/05/nyregion/measles-emergency-new-york-state-hochul.html) - [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/6/new-york-state-declares-measles-disaster-amid-resurgence-of-disease-in-us?traffic_source=rss)

### U.S. Households Pay 62% More for Drinking Water Than a Decade Ago

A Food & Water Watch analysis of 2025 billing data from the 500 largest U.S. community water systems found that households pay 62% more for drinking water than they did in 2015. The systems analyzed serve about 155 million people, or 45% of the country’s population. The study did not include wastewater or stormwater charges.

[The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/oct/06/households-drinking-water-cost-study)

### Trump Approves Firing-Squad Execution for Fort Hood Shooter

President Trump approved the U.S. Army firing-squad execution of Nidal Hasan, who was sentenced to death by a military jury for killing 13 people at Fort Hood in 2009. Hasan would become the first U.S. soldier executed by the military in more than 60 years, according to The Guardian.

[The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/oct/05/trump-fort-hood-shooter-execution) - [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cq8ezkkkrpy1o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

### Treasury Yields Ease as Investors Reconsider Recent Bond Selloff

U.S. Treasury yields moved lower as investors considered whether the recent bond selloff had gone too far and whether current levels offered attractive opportunities to buy. Long-dated yields had remained near multidecade highs amid concerns about fiscal conditions, inflation and debt sustainability.

[The Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/finance/investing/u-s-long-end-treasury-yields-remain-elevated-three-year-auction-awaited-bf72da8d?mod=rss_markets_main)

### Oil Prices Fall as Middle Eastern Exports Recover

Oil prices declined as traders weighed recovering crude exports against shipping incidents near the Strait of Hormuz and increased tensions around the Red Sea. U.S. benchmark West Texas Intermediate fell below $90 a barrel in early trading, according to The Wall Street Journal.

### Informa Agrees to Buy Clarion Events for $2.96 Billion

Researchers studying the Voynich manuscript argue that its pages may have been incorrectly bound. The medieval manuscript contains botanical drawings, astronomical diagrams, images of nude women and undeciphered text, and its meaning has remained unresolved for centuries.
