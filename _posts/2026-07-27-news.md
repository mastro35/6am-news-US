### U.S. and Iran Pause Military Strikes, Igniting Diplomatic Hopes and Dropping Oil Prices

The United States and Iran have paused their respective military strikes, providing a much-needed reprieve after nearly two weeks of escalating conflict. The cessation of attacks has raised hopes among international observers for a diplomatic resolution and the reopening of the crucial Strait of Hormuz. Top U.S. military officials reportedly advised Donald Trump to halt the bombing campaign, citing concerns over dwindling stocks of air-defense munitions and the limits of the campaign's effectiveness. While mediation efforts continue, the temporary truce has brought relief to global financial markets, sending stock futures higher and easing immediate fears of a wider Middle Eastern war.

### Three Killed and Four Injured in Shooting at Seattle Food Festival

Three people were killed and four others injured, including a two-year-old boy, when gunfire erupted at the "Bite of Seattle" food festival near the city's iconic Space Needle. Local police responded to the scene on Sunday evening and quickly took one suspect into custody, while continuing to search for other potential leads. The shooting caused widespread panic among festival attendees, with vendor livestreams capturing the chaotic moments as shots rang out. Seattle Mayor Katie Wilson condemned the incident as an "act of horrific violence" and praised the rapid response of law enforcement and emergency services. Authorities have not yet released a motive or the identities of the victims and the suspect, but have urged the public to avoid the Seattle Center area while the investigation remains active.

### Netanyahu Accuses NYC Mayor Mamdani of Fomenting Hate Over Arrest Threat

Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has accused New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani of "fomenting hate" ahead of his planned visit to the United States. The clash follows a video address released by Mayor Mamdani last week, in which he called on the federal government to arrest Netanyahu on war crimes charges if he travels to New York for the United Nations General Assembly in September. Although Mamdani later acknowledged that New York City lacks the legal authority to execute arrest warrants issued by the International Criminal Court, his statements drew fierce criticism from Netanyahu, who labeled the war crimes allegations "bogus." Despite the tension, Netanyahu reaffirmed his intention to address the UN gathering, while U.S. political figures remain divided over his upcoming visit.

### Berlin Pride Islamist Terror Attack Suspect Shot Dead by German Police

### Meta Secured Massive Louisiana Data Center Project Through Secret Talks

The planned development is project-scaled to cover nearly six square miles, making it one of the largest technology hubs in the country. ### Misleading AI-Generated TikTok Doctors Pose Public Safety Threat

Medical experts are warning of a "huge danger to public safety" after research revealed that AI-generated doctors are accumulating millions of views on TikTok by spreading unverified health advice. These highly realistic deepfake profiles often mimic professional medical authorities to peddle dubious health claims, promote "miracle cures," and spread dangerous medical myths. Dr. Emma Runswick, deputy chair of the British Medical Association council, flagged the severe risks these accounts pose, noting they exploit vulnerable users seeking health advice. The rise of these automated accounts has prompted calls for social media platforms to implement stricter verification measures and crack down on AI-generated medical misinformation.

### Tadej Pogačar Wins Tour de France to Claim Record-Equalling Fifth Title

Slovenian cyclist Tadej Pogačar has won the Tour de France, drawing him level with cycling legends Eddy Merckx, Bernard Hinault, Jacques Anquetil, and Miguel Induráin with a record-equalling fifth career title. Pogačar dominated this year's race, securing a virtually insurmountable lead early on. The final stage on the Champs-Élysées ended in dramatic fashion as Mathieu van der Poel clinched the stage victory by a tire's width, following a late breakaway alongside Pogačar. At just 27 years old, Pogačar is the youngest rider in history to reach five Tour victories, cementing his legacy as one of the greatest cyclists of all time.

### Luke Littler Retains World Matchplay Darts Title with Record-Breaking Win

Teenage sensation Luke Littler successfully defended his World Matchplay darts title in Blackpool with a dominant 18-9 victory over Gerwyn Price. The 19-year-old put on a masterclass performance, averaging 111.53 in the final to set a new tournament-long average record of 111.04, eclipsing Phil Taylor's long-standing record of 106.31. Littler also broke his own competition record for 180s, hitting 12 in the final to reach 66 for the week. With this victory, Littler becomes only the third man in history to successfully defend the Matchplay title, further establishing himself as the dominant force in professional darts.
