### Peggy Flanagan wins Minnesota Democratic Senate primary

Minnesota Lt. Gov. Peggy Flanagan defeated Rep. Angie Craig in the Democratic Senate primary. She could become the first Native woman in the Senate.

[The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/11/us/politics/minnesota-democratic-senate-primary-flanagan-craig.html) - [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/aug/11/minnesota-peggy-flanagan-wins-primary)
