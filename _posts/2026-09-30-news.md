### Federal Oversight of Oakland Police Ends After 23 Years

A federal judge released the Oakland Police Department from court oversight after 23 years, ending the longest such arrangement in U.S. history. The department was placed under a reform program after officers known as the “riders” were accused of planting evidence, falsifying reports and using excessive force against primarily young Black men.

[The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/sep/29/oakland-police-federal-oversight-ends)
