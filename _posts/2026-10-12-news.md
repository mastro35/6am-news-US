President Donald Trump said Russia and Ukraine had agreed to halt attacks on energy infrastructure, but Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said the announcement initially caught him by surprise and Kyiv would support a truce if Moscow did. ### Indian opposition plans march to Supreme Court over election rolls
India’s opposition parties planned a march to the Supreme Court to demand the resignation of chief election commissioner Gyanesh Kumar. ### Pakistan suspends opposition-led provincial government
Pakistan’s president declared a state of emergency in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and suspended its government, led by former Prime Minister Imran Khan’s Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf party. The federal government said provincial authorities had failed to address Islamist militancy; a party spokesperson called the move a coup disguised as an emergency.

### At least 139 dead after migrant boat capsizes near Djibouti
A boat carrying 305 people from Djibouti toward Yemen capsized near the port town of Obock, killing at least 139 people, the United Nations said. Dozens remained missing, and the bodies of 15 children were recovered, according to the International Organization for Migration.
