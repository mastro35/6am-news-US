### Thousands remain without power in Indiana nearly two weeks after storms

### Study finds toxic chemicals in many personal-care products
A peer-reviewed study testing more than 110 products, including shampoo and baby lotion, found evidence of unlabeled toxic chemicals in about 85% of them. The products contained, on average, more than three times as many chemicals as listed on their labels. Even 26% of products marketed as “eco,” “green” or “natural” contained at least one dangerous compound.

[The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/aug/23/personal-care-products-unlabeled-toxic-chemicals)
