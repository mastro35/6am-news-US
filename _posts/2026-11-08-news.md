### Houston mayor limits when police can contact ICE
Houston Mayor John Whitmire has directed police to contact federal immigration authorities only when a person faces charges that could lead to jail time. The move reverses the city’s previous approach and follows months of public outcry after an ICE officer fatally shot a man in the city.
