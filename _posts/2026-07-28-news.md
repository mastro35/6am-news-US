### Major 7.1-Magnitude Earthquake Strikes Southern Japan, Spurring Tsunami Warning

A powerful earthquake, measured at magnitude 7.1 by the Japan Meteorological Agency and 6.8 by the USGS, struck off the coast of Japan's southern Kyushu region on Tuesday. The quake, centered near the coastal city of Uto in Kumamoto Prefecture, prompted local authorities to issue immediate tsunami warnings for low-lying coastal areas. Residents were urged to evacuate to higher ground. Although the seismic event caused widespread panic and disrupted local transit systems, there were no immediate reports of severe casualties or structural damage.

## Culture & Entertainment

### Betye Saar, Legendary Assemblage Artist of the Black Arts Movement, Dies at 99

Renowned artist Betye Saar, a towering figure in the Black Arts Movement, has died at her home in Los Angeles at the age of 99. Saar was celebrated for her mastery of assemblage, a style of sculpture created from everyday found objects. She achieved international acclaim for her powerful work reclaiming racially stereotypical Jim Crow-era memorabilia and reframing it as symbols of Black liberation and strength. Throughout her decades-long career, Saar explored deep themes of family, history, and spirituality, securing her legacy as a transformative force in American contemporary art.
