## Crime & Local News

### Salman Rushdie's Attacker Convicted of Terrorism Charges

A federal jury convicted Hadi Matar of attempting to provide material support to a foreign terrorist organization in connection with his 2022 attack on author Salman Rushdie. Matar stabbed Rushdie multiple times at a New York literary event, leaving the author blind in one eye. The conviction carries a potential life sentence, marking a significant legal milestone in addressing state-sponsored and radicalized violence against public figures.

### Ballistics Discrepancy Found in Fatal Seattle Food Festival Shooting

Police in Seattle revealed that bullets recovered from two fatal victims at a weekend food festival shootout do not match the weapon seized from a 15-year-old suspect arrested at the scene. The investigation remains active as authorities search for additional suspects.

### Federal Reserve Holds Interest Rates Steady as Treasury Yields Surge

The Federal Reserve opted to maintain its benchmark interest rate between 3.5% and 3.75% for the fifth consecutive meeting, signaling caution amid persistent inflationary pressures. The decision exposed internal divisions, with three officials dissenting in favor of a rate hike. Following the announcement and remarks by Chairman Kevin Warsh, long-term borrowing costs surged, with the 30-year Treasury yield hitting a 19-year high of nearly 5.24%.
