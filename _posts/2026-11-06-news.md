## Front Page

### Democrats’ midterm gains deepen as party expands House lead
Democrats widened their advantage in the House, flipping seats in Ohio, Arizona and North Carolina and leading in 253 races to Republicans’ 157, with 25 contests still uncalled, according to the latest results cited by *The Guardian*. The closely watched Senate races in Mississippi and Alaska also remained unresolved. The scale of the gains is intensifying debate over the political consequences of the election and the future direction of both parties. [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/live/2026/nov/06/us-midterm-elections-2026-democrats-blue-wave-congress-latest-news-updates)

### Newsom commutes sentences for California’s 548 death-row prisoners
California Gov. Gavin Newsom commuted the sentences of all 548 people on the state’s death row to life in prison without the possibility of parole. The move is one of the largest acts of clemency in U.S. history and comes as Newsom prepares to leave office in January. California has long had the nation’s largest death-row population. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqrkyyd2j8mvo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss) - [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/6/california-governor-commutes-sentences-of-more-than-500-people-on-death-row?traffic_source=rss)

## Politics

### Latino voters’ anger over immigration raids shaped midterm backlash
Some Latino voters who had supported Donald Trump said aggressive immigration enforcement, including raids affecting relatives, employees and neighbors, helped drive them back to the polls against Republicans. The *Guardian* reports that Hispanic turnout was especially consequential in key battlegrounds, with voters describing the enforcement campaign as a direct threat to their communities. [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/nov/06/latino-voters-midterms-republicans-trump)

### Nithya Raman defeats Karen Bass in Los Angeles mayoral race
City Council member Nithya Raman defeated incumbent Karen Bass in a close Los Angeles mayoral race. Raman, a Democratic Socialist, has pledged to address homelessness and housing affordability. Her win adds to progressive gains in the midterms and makes her the city’s first South Asian mayor, according to Al Jazeera. [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/05/us/la-mayor-raman-bass.html) - [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/5/nithya-raman-wins-la-mayoral-race-becoming-citys-first-south-asian-mayor?traffic_source=rss)

### Judge pauses construction of Trump’s proposed Washington arch
A federal judge issued a 14-day order blocking construction work on President Trump’s proposed 250-foot arch in Washington, hours after a federal planning commission approved the project. The ruling is a temporary setback for a centerpiece of Trump’s plans to reshape the capital; the project faces further legal challenges. [The Associated Press via The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/nov/06/judge-temporarily-halts-work-trump-triumphal-arch-washington) - [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cq99zd93ryvko?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss) - [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/6/us-judge-halts-construction-of-trumps-triumphal-arch-in-washington-dc?traffic_source=rss)
