### Navi Pillay Wins the 2026 Nobel Peace Prize

South African jurist Navi Pillay has won the 2026 Nobel Peace Prize for efforts to promote peace, international law and accountability. Pillay has served as a judge in South Africa and on international courts investigating war crimes. ### Hurricane Isaias Could Test FEMA After Staffing Cuts

Gulf officials say they are prepared for Hurricane Isaias, but emergency-management experts warn the storm could be the first major test of FEMA’s ability to deploy a large surge workforce after staffing cuts and agency restructuring. More than 4,300 employees left FEMA in fiscal year 2025, representing about 17% of its workforce, according to a Government Accountability Office report cited by The Guardian.
