## Front Page

### Typhoon Dolphin Drives Mass Evacuations in China

Typhoon Dolphin, described as China’s strongest storm of the year, made landfall on the country’s eastern coast, bringing torrential rain and strong winds. More than one million people were moved to safety, while authorities warned of flooding and landslides. The storm had also affected Japan’s Okinawa prefecture and parts of the Philippines.

[The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/10/world/asia/typhoon-dolphin-china-evacuations.html) - [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/aug/10/typhoon-dolphin-china-shanghai-flooding-evacuations)

### Ukrainian Drone Strike Kills at Least 13 in Russia’s Tatarstan

Officials said a Ukrainian drone strike on an industrial center in Russia’s Tatarstan region killed at least 13 people. The attack was described as one of the deadliest Ukrainian strikes on Russian territory since Moscow began its war against Ukraine.

[The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/10/world/europe/ukrainian-drone-attack-kills-13-deep-inside-russia.html) - [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cvgjvgv926po?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

### Uncertainty Over Strait of Hormuz Keeps Oil Markets on Edge

Oil prices rose as doubts grew over when shipping through the Strait of Hormuz could resume. Iran has tied reopening the strategic waterway to U.S. concessions on several demands, while hopes for a near-term agreement remained limited.

[The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/09/business/oil-stocks-iran-war.html) - [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/8/10/oil-prices-climb-as-iranian-demands-cloud-outlook-for-strait-of-hormuz?traffic_source=rss)

### Lake Mead Falls to Its Lowest Recorded Level

Lake Mead’s water level has dropped below its previous record low, reaching 1,040.4 feet above sea level, according to federal data. The reservoir’s continued decline threatens water supplies for millions and could reduce electricity generation from Hoover Dam.

[The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/09/us/lake-mead-water-level-record-low.html) - [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/aug/09/lake-mead-record-low-water-level-colorado-river)

### Wildfires Force Thousands to Flee British Columbia

A fast-moving wildfire in British Columbia has burned more than 33,000 acres, remained out of control and prompted evacuation orders for about 20,000 people. The fires have killed one person, according to the report.
