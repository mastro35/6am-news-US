### Trump’s account of call with Fed chief conflicts with calendar records

Trump said he spoke with Federal Reserve Chair Kevin Warsh about how to vote at a meeting, but Warsh’s September calendar contains no record of the conversation. The discrepancy raises questions about the president’s contact with the central bank, whose independence is central to setting monetary policy. [The Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2026/11/09/fed-kevin-warsh-calendar-shows-no-trump-calls-trump-says-they-talked/)

### Oil prices ease as investors watch Iran tensions and U.S. inflation

Oil prices fell and remained below $100 a barrel after Trump played down the prospect of new strikes on Iran. Attacks on Saudi Arabia and fresh incidents involving tankers in the Strait of Hormuz continued to pose risks to supply. Investors were also looking ahead to U.S. inflation data. [The Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/world/middle-east/oil-prices-fall-staying-below-100-as-trump-plays-down-new-iran-strikes-27dc3911)

### Medicare Advantage premiums fall, but other costs may offset savings
