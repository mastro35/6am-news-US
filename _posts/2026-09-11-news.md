## National News

### Missouri UPS worker killed less than two weeks before retirement

Paul Roberts, 63, a UPS employee for 44 years, was struck and killed by a company vehicle while working at the company’s customer care center in Earth City, Missouri, authorities said. The death has prompted a federal labor investigation and an outpouring of grief from Roberts’ family and colleagues.

[The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/sep/11/ups-worker-killed-on-job-weeks-before-retirement)

### Hurricane Lowell causes widespread damage across western Hawaii

Hurricane Lowell passed about 50 miles from Ni‘ihau as a Category 2 storm, producing winds of up to 110 mph and widespread power outages across Ni‘ihau, Kauaʻi and O‘ahu. A tornado watch was also issued for Kauaʻi, which the report described as likely the first in Hawaii’s history, although no tornado was documented.

[The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/sep/11/weather-tracker-hurricane-lowell-pounds-hawaii-western-islands)

### New World screwworm detected in a Texas ranch horse

A working ranch horse in Presidio County, Texas, has been infected with New World screwworm, marking the first reported U.S. equine infection in the outbreak. Authorities are racing to contain the flesh-eating parasite, which had previously been largely eradicated.

[The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/sep/10/texas-horse-screwworm-infection)

## Business & Finance

### Oil prices remain above $100 as Middle East supply risks mount

Oil prices are on track for weekly gains of about 10% after rising above $100 a barrel. Traders are assessing growing risks to Middle East crude exports, while U.S. diesel prices have reached a record high. The rise in energy costs is also contributing to concerns about inflation and interest rates.

[The Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/finance/commodities-futures/oil-rises-on-escalating-supply-disruption-concerns-998e07a1?mod=rss_markets_main) - [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/09/11/business/diesel-fuel-prices.html)

### Bond yields ease as investors await U.S. inflation data

U.S. stock futures moved slightly higher and Treasury yields edged lower ahead of key consumer-price data. The 10-year Treasury yield moved away from the 5% level, but investors remained cautious as oil prices and expectations of higher interest rates continued to pressure markets.

[The Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/finance/stocks/stock-futures-nudge-up-yields-slip-but-markets-remain-on-edge-035097c4?mod=rss_markets_main)

### Businesses prepare for disruption as Salvadoran TPS deadline approaches

Restaurants, hospitals and construction companies have begun preparing for layoffs as the end of temporary protected status looms for 232,000 Salvadorans. The businesses are bracing for labor shortages and broader disruption if workers lose their protection.
