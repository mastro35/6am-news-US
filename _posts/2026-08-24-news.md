### Reno wildfire forces thousands to evacuate
The fast-moving Hawk fire in Reno, Nevada, has burned more than 10,500 acres and pushed toward the city center and the University of Nevada campus. Nearly 14,000 homes were placed under evacuation orders as strong winds, low humidity and dry vegetation fueled the blaze. The governor declared a state of emergency.
