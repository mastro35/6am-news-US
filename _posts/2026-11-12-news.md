### Nvidia’s board weighs Jensen Huang’s future amid reported China-linked espionage concerns

Nvidia’s board is reportedly considering whether Jensen Huang should remain CEO after he acknowledged a relationship with a woman U.S. officials reportedly believe was recruited by Chinese intelligence. ## Culture & Entertainment

### Historic SS United States is sunk off Florida to become an artificial reef

The SS United States, once the world’s fastest ocean liner, was deliberately sunk off Florida’s coast to create what is expected to be the world’s largest artificial reef. The 1952 vessel set a transatlantic speed record on its maiden voyage and carried passengers for 17 years. Some advocates had hoped to preserve it as a museum.

### Met exhibition traces postwar Japan through photographers’ eyes

The Metropolitan Museum of Art in New York is opening “A Long Shadow: Postwar Japan Through the Lens,” its first exhibition of Japanese photography in more than three decades. The show brings together 15 artists and works including prints, books, magazines and video exploring how photographers documented Japan in the decades after World War II. It runs through May 2, 2027.
