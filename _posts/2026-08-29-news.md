## Front Page

### Nepal flood disaster leaves hundreds dead and thousands missing

Rescue teams in Nepal are digging through mud-filled hydropower tunnels where people are believed to be trapped. The death toll from the flash floods has risen to 626 in Nepal, with seven confirmed dead in Tibet, while the number of missing has climbed to almost 3,000. Bad weather continues to hamper rescue efforts, and officials are seeking technical assistance and billions of dollars for recovery.

[The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/live/2026/aug/29/nepal-tibet-floods-latest-updates-death-toll-missing-search-and-rescue)

### Trump announces U.S. agreement to control much of Venezuela’s oil reserves

President Donald Trump said the United States will have “majority” control of 65 billion barrels of Venezuela’s proven oil reserves through a partnership with private businesses. The terms of the agreement remain unclear. Venezuela’s interim president said the deal would help revive the country’s economy.

[The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/aug/28/trump-venezuela-oil-agreement) - [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/28/business/trump-venezuela-oil-deal.html)

### U.S. military planners worry Iran war is draining munitions

The United States has rushed so many munitions to the Middle East during the war with Iran that planners are concerned the reduced stockpile could weaken the military’s ability to deter or respond to potential threats from China and Russia.
