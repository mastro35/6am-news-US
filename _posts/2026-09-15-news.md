### Supreme Court Blocks Trump’s Mail-Ballot Restrictions
The Supreme Court rejected the Trump administration’s effort to impose new requirements on mail-in ballots ahead of the midterm elections. The ruling allows states to continue sending ballots under the processes they have used for years and avoids a potentially chaotic last-minute change.

[The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/09/14/us/politics/supreme-court-trump-mail-voting.html) - [The Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/09/14/supreme-court-blocks-sweeping-mail-ballot-restrictions-midterm-elections/)

### Houthi Advances Raise Fears of a New Oil Supply Crisis
Houthi militants in Yemen seized the Greater and Lesser Hanish islands in the Red Sea, near the Bab al-Mandab Strait. The move strengthens the Iran-backed group’s ability to influence a key shipping route as attacks and supply concerns push oil prices higher.

[The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/sep/15/houthi-rebels-seize-red-sea-hanish-islands-saudi-oil-warning)

### NATO Jets Shoot Down Drone Over Lithuania
NATO fighter jets shot down a drone that entered Lithuanian airspace. Authorities said its origin was unknown but that it likely entered from neighboring Belarus, a Russian ally. Poland also scrambled fighter jets amid reports of Russian drone activity.

[The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/sep/15/nato-jets-shoot-down-drone-violating-lithuania-airspace-kaunas) - [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cjx2zgk7edlzo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

### Kyrgyzstan’s Economy Booms as Trade With Russia Surges
Kyrgyzstan’s economy grew 11% last year, partly because the Central Asian nation became a key conduit for goods flowing into Russia. The country has become an important middleman for Russian imports from China since the start of the war in Ukraine.

[The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/09/15/world/asia/kyrgyzstan-economy-russia-war.html)

### Ebola Outbreak in Congo Appears to Have Peaked
Authorities in the Democratic Republic of Congo said the Ebola outbreak has reached its peak, with infections slowing for the first time since the epidemic was declared in May. Almost 3,500 people have died, and experts said more work is needed to ensure the outbreak is under control.

[The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/global-development/2026/sep/15/ebola-outbreak-in-drc-has-peaked-say-authorities-as-infection-rates-slow)
