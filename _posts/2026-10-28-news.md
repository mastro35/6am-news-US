## Front Page

### Deadly collision between two trains in Spain leaves at least 10 dead
At least 10 people were killed when one train struck the rear of another in Spain’s Toledo province Wednesday morning, authorities said. Two carriages derailed and went down an embankment. Rail services between Madrid and the southern region of Andalusia were suspended as emergency crews responded and officials began gathering information about the crash. [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/oct/28/spain-train-crash-high-speed-toledo) - [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/live/2026/10/28/world/spain-train-crash-toledo/spain-train-crash-toledo)

### Israel election leaves Netanyahu and opposition short of a clear path to power
Early results and exit polls showed no bloc with the 61 seats needed to form a government, setting the stage for coalition negotiations. Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu’s Likud improved as votes were counted, while Arab parties appeared poised for gains that could make them pivotal. The final outcome remained uncertain. ### Missing Arizona woman’s family faces new uncertainty after mistaken report she was found
Authorities in Arizona say Mexican police were wrong to report that Shayna Feinman had been found alive in Tijuana. Feinman disappeared in rural Yavapai County in March 2024, when she was 38. The announcement had raised hopes for her family, whose search has now returned to uncertainty. ### Grand jury declines to indict former White House aide Cassidy Hutchinson

## Economy & Finance

### Federal Reserve expected to hold rates steady ahead of midterms
The Federal Reserve is widely expected to leave interest rates unchanged at its Wednesday meeting, according to reports published before the decision. The central bank raised rates last month for the first time since 2023, citing persistent inflation, which has remained above its 2% target. [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/28/business/federal-reserve-interest-rates.html) - [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/business/2026/oct/28/fed-interest-rates-midterms)

### Oil prices climb as traders weigh risk to Middle East supplies
Oil rose as markets factored in a greater risk of disruption after Yemen’s Houthi militants said they had targeted Saudi Arabia’s Ras Tanura refinery. The price move came despite diplomatic efforts in the region, as traders focused on the potential effect of further escalation on supply. [The Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/world/middle-east/oil-prices-rise-as-houthi-militants-target-saudi-energy-45c38fe0?mod=rss_worldnews)

### Higher mortgage rates and tight housing supply squeeze renters
Higher mortgage costs and slower home construction are strengthening landlords’ position in the rental market, adding pressure on tenants. The New York Times reports that the housing squeeze is intensifying as would-be buyers face elevated borrowing costs and fewer new homes come online. [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/28/business/housing-market-rent-pressure.html)
