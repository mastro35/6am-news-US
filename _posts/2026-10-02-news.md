### Flydubai pilot recounts cockpit attack that sent plane into steep descent

### California bans child marriage

California has outlawed child marriage, joining 17 other states where the practice is banned. Under the previous state law, minors of any age could obtain a court order to marry with parental consent. An advocacy group that supported the legislation said 9,000 children are married in California each year.

[The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/oct/01/california-outlaws-child-marriage)

### Tennessee pauses executions after failed lethal injection

Tennessee’s governor suspended all executions and ordered an independent inquiry after Christa Pike survived two attempts to execute her by lethal injection.
