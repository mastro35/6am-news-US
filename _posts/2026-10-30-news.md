## Front Page

### Binance investigates a suspected $6.3 billion crypto heist
The world’s largest cryptocurrency exchange suspended some transactions after a security breach that analysts estimate may have cost as much as $6.3 billion. Investigators cited by several outlets suspect a North Korean hacking group; Binance says it will cover users’ losses, while efforts to freeze the stolen funds continue. ### Early voting tops 43 million as midterm campaigns enter final stretch
More than 43 million ballots have been cast ahead of the midterms, with early voting in some battleground counties outpacing previous elections. Hispanic voters have contributed to the surge, according to the BBC. Meanwhile, President Trump canceled planned rallies in Kansas and Iowa and is expected to address voters remotely. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c30rlxkjv2yko) - [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/29/us/elections/trump-rally-canceled-kansas-iowa.html)

### U.S. oil companies reap more than $130 billion during Iran war
U.S. companies that produce and refine oil made more than $130 billion in profit during the Iran war—nearly three times their earnings over the same period last year, according to an analysis by The New York Times. ExxonMobil and Chevron also reported exceptionally strong quarterly results as supply disruptions lifted refining margins and global demand for U.S. fuel. [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/30/business/energy-environment/exxon-chevron-oil-profits-iran-war.html) - [The Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/business/energy-oil/exxonmobil-xom-chevron-cvx-3q-earnings-report-2026-stock-1d890682?mod=pls_whats_news_us_business_f)

### Government corruption emerges as a major midterm concern, poll finds
Nearly nine in 10 Americans surveyed said corruption—defined in the poll as officials putting personal or donors’ financial interests ahead of the public—is a problem. The Harris poll for The Guardian found 76% believe public officials care more about enriching themselves than helping ordinary Americans. [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/oct/30/government-corruption-us-midterm-poll)

### Democrats rally behind Michigan Senate candidate Abdul El-Sayed
Democratic leaders are increasingly backing Abdul El-Sayed, the party’s progressive Senate nominee in Michigan, after a divisive nomination contest. The shift comes as President Trump has attacked El-Sayed with false and Islamophobic claims, and as the closely watched race is seen as a test of the party’s progressive wing. [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/oct/30/abdul-el-sayed-michigan-senate-tump) - [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/30/us/politics/michigan-senate-democratic-presidential-primary.html)

### Trump’s deportation policies could reshape the Texas border vote

Shares in several major technology companies came under pressure after quarterly results revived investor concerns about the cost and payoff of artificial-intelligence investments. The broader S&P 500 held up better than some leading tech stocks, while investors sought safer assets and Treasury yields fell. ### High-cost cash advances are trapping some small businesses in debt
Merchant cash advances offer businesses quick access to funds, but some carry effective financing costs as high as 2,000%, The Wall Street Journal reports. The arrangements can drain revenue through frequent repayments, leaving small businesses struggling to escape the debt. [The Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/business/entrepreneurship/business-finance-merchant-cash-advance-37d8bc42?mod=rss_markets_main)

### Amazon posts strong cloud growth, but cash-flow decline unsettles investors
Amazon’s third-quarter net income topped $20 billion, and its cloud business recorded its strongest growth in years. But free cash flow plunged, and the company’s results contributed to investor concerns about the scale and returns of Big Tech’s AI spending. ### Spanish train collision leaves at least 69 dead
At least 69 people were confirmed dead after a high-speed train collision in Spain, with authorities warning the toll could rise and dozens still reported missing. Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez said victims or injured passengers may include people from 27 nationalities. Investigators are examining the crash, while the disaster has intensified scrutiny of railway safety. [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/live/2026/oct/30/spain-investigates-toledo-crash-pedro-sanchez-france-italy-protests-latest-news-updates) - [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cmqxnl312v34o)

### Ukraine resumes strikes on Russian oil refinery after two-week pause
Ukraine struck a Russian oil refinery, ending a two-week halt in attacks that had followed pressure from Washington to stop such operations. The strike came as Russia reported intercepting more than 1,000 Ukrainian drones across several regions overnight. [The Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/world/europe/ukraine-hits-russian-oil-refinery-ending-a-pause-and-defying-trump-be68eeff?mod=rss_worldnews) - [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/10/30/russian-oil-refinery-struck-as-ukraine-renews-drone-attacks)

Benjamin Netanyahu’s coalition lost its majority, while opposition parties could potentially form one if they reach an agreement with Arab-led parties. [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/30/world/europe/israel-election-netanyahu-eisenkot.html) - [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/oct/30/friday-briefing-israel-has-a-dilemma-can-its-opposition-govern-with-palestinian-parties)
