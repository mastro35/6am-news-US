### Germany’s Merz Visits Kyiv as Russia Strikes the Ukrainian Capital

German Chancellor Friedrich Merz began an unannounced visit to Kyiv as Russian strikes continued. A strike hit the city’s Northern Bridge about 20 minutes from the station where Merz arrived. ### Investigation Expands Into Flydubai Cockpit Attack

Authorities are investigating the background of a Flydubai co-pilot accused of attacking the captain and attempting to take control of an Israel-bound flight. The flight carried 182 people and plunged more than 16,000 feet before passengers and crew intervened.

### Cornell President Promises Greater Transparency After Rape Allegations

Cornell President Michael Kotlikoff said the university “must do better” and promised a serious review of fraternities and sororities after allegations that a student was drugged and sexually assaulted at a fraternity house. He said the university would improve transparency, hold Greek organizations accountable and strengthen sexual-assault prevention. He also backed New York Gov. Kathy Hochul’s decision to appoint a special prosecutor for a reopened criminal investigation.

### Tennessee Prison Chief to Leave After Failed Execution

Tennessee Department of Correction Commissioner Frank Strada will step down later this month as the state begins an independent investigation into the failed lethal injection of death-row prisoner Christa Pike. Pike survived two lethal injections and remains in critical condition, according to the feed. The governor has halted scheduled executions while the review proceeds.

[The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/03/us/tennessee-prison-commissioner-resigns-christa-pike.html) - [The Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/nation/2026/10/03/tennessee-prisons-chief-resigns-after-failed-christa-pike-execution-gov-says/)

### Debris Found From Missing Medical Aircraft Near Nantucket

The U.S. Coast Guard found debris off Nantucket Island from a medical transport aircraft that lost contact during a flight from Bermuda to Boston. Six people were aboard. Search-and-rescue teams were looking for those on the aircraft after the plane dropped significantly in altitude, according to flight data.

## Tech & Society

### California Approves New Limits on Workplace AI

California has enacted laws aimed at protecting workers from artificial intelligence. The measures prohibit employers from relying entirely on AI to decide whether to fire workers, using AI to predict employees’ emotional states or collecting neural data. Companies must also notify workers if AI contributed to layoffs, and AI surveillance is prohibited in workplace bathrooms.

[The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/oct/03/california-ai-laws-worker-protection)

### White House AI Task Force Will Examine Technology’s Risks

A new White House task force will have 120 days to report on artificial intelligence risks and determine what responsibility the federal government should have. The group will also examine the technology’s opportunities and focus on keeping the United States first in the field.
