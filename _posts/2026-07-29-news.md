## Front Page

### Race Against Time in Japan After Devastating Kyushu Earthquake

A powerful 6.8-magnitude earthquake struck the southern Japanese island of Kyushu, leaving at least 13 people dead and hundreds injured. The earthquake registered a maximum 7 on the Japanese intensity scale, flattening older wooden homes, knocking out power, and severing water lines to tens of thousands of residents. Search and rescue teams are currently working through rubble and collapsed structures, including a local shopping mall, in what Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi called a "race against time" to locate survivors. With temperatures in the region forecast to reach 95°F (35°C), many displaced residents spent the night in their cars, terrified of the constant aftershocks.

## Crime & Local News

### Wisconsin Residents Pick Through Wreckage Following Powerful EF-2 Tornado

Emergency crews and local residents are assessing the extensive damage left behind by a devastating EF-2 tornado that swept through northeastern Wisconsin's Fox Cities region. The cities of Appleton, Menasha, and Neenah bore the brunt of the storm's fury, which flattened homes, flipped vehicles, and snapped utility poles. Remarkably, local authorities have reported no fatalities or serious injuries, though tens of thousands of residents remain without power. First responders continue to search through structurally compromised buildings to ensure all community members are accounted for.

Colt Gray, the 16-year-old teenager responsible for the deadly 2024 shooting at Apalachee High School near Atlanta, Georgia, has been sentenced to life in prison without the possibility of parole. Clayton, a close ally of Donald Trump, secured unanimous support from Republican lawmakers, while every voting Democrat opposed the confirmation. During his contentious confirmation hearings, Clayton faced intense questioning from opposition lawmakers regarding his lack of traditional intelligence experience, as well as his refusal to explicitly state that Joe Biden won the 2020 presidential election. Clayton takes over the leadership of the nation's intelligence apparatus from Bill Pulte.

### Southern Europe Braces for Intense Heatwave Amid Escalating Wildfire Risks

Southern and western Europe are bracing for a fresh heatwave, with meteorologists predicting temperatures to surpass 104°F (40°C) across wide swaths of France and Spain. The severe weather is threatening to undo progress made by firefighters battling massive forest blazes in regions like Gironde in France and around Madrid in Spain. High winds and dryness have already prompted authorities to evacuate thousands of tourists from campgrounds along France's Atlantic coast. Climate experts warn that the combination of wet winters followed by extreme summer heat is creating a tinderbox effect, making European wildfires increasingly frequent and intense.

### Torrential Rain Causes Chaos at Commonwealth Games as Amy Hunt Claims Silver

Day six of the 2026 Commonwealth Games in Glasgow was severely disrupted by torrential rain, turning the athletic track at Scotstoun Stadium into waterlogged pools.
