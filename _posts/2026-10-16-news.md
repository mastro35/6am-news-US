## Front Page

### Oregon school shooting kills three children and a teacher
A 16-year-old opened fire at Silver Rail Elementary School in Bend, killing three children, ages 5, 8 and 11, and a teacher, authorities said. A community member shot and killed the suspect. Two other people were hospitalized in critical condition; police have not announced a motive.

## National News

### California wildfires prompt evacuations as heat and winds persist
Several fires broke out across California as officials warned that strong winds and high temperatures could fuel their spread. The largest, the Henderson fire at Camp Pendleton near San Diego, burned more than 1,000 acres. Evacuations were ordered, and a section of Interstate 5 was briefly closed.

[The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/oct/15/wildfire-california-high-temperatures-strong-winds)

### ICE agents pursuing a man in Houston struck a worker, family says
Immigration and Customs Enforcement agents rammed a man during a pursuit in Houston, leaving him with a shattered leg. The family of the person agents were seeking said a bulletin showed the target had died in 2017.

[The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/15/us/ice-pursuit-houston-dead-man.html)

### Police identify man found in a barrel at Lake Mead
Authorities identified the homicide victim whose remains were found in a barrel at Lake Mead in 2022 as Edward Lelburian Jackson. An initial autopsy indicated he had been killed decades earlier; investigators had been working to establish his identity.

[The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/oct/15/edward-lelburian-jackson-barrel-lake-mead)

### Justice Department sues California over ballots tied to alleged noncitizens

### Poor Indian sugarcane crop raises concerns about global supply
A weak sugarcane harvest in India, the world’s second-largest sugar producer, is affecting farmers and consumers and putting pressure on the country’s energy-security goals. The New York Times reports that El Niño-related weather risks are threatening the broader sugar supply.

[The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/16/climate/india-sugarcane-production-sugar-prices.html)

### Indonesian stocks plunge after MSCI warns of possible index removal

### Trump criticizes Ukraine as Zelensky seeks European support
President Donald Trump called Ukraine’s renewed strikes on Russian oil refineries a “disgrace” and said President Volodymyr Zelensky was more difficult to deal with than Vladimir Putin. Zelensky was in Brussels seeking support from European leaders, while European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen said the EU would not lift sanctions unless Russia stopped the war.

### Flash floods in Nepal kill at least five and destroy villages
A landslide blocked the Budhi Gandaki River in Nepal’s Gorkha district, triggering downstream flooding that killed at least five people. Nearly 100 people were rescued, two villages were reportedly destroyed, and more than 650 people were affected, according to disaster authorities.

[The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/environment/2026/oct/16/flash-floods-deaths-nepal-high-temperatures-portugal)

### Venezuelan capital feels safer after deadly police raids
Crime in Caracas has fallen after police raids that killed thousands, according to The New York Times. Venezuela is withdrawing from the International Criminal Court as the court investigates the campaign.

[The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/16/world/americas/caracas-is-eerily-safe-state-death-squads-made-sure-of-it.html)

### Houthi missile strike on Yemen presidential palace kills five
Missiles fired by Yemen’s Houthi movement hit the presidential palace complex in Aden, killing five people, the government said. The prime minister and other ministers were unharmed, according to the information minister.
