### Trump Moves to Restrict Birthright Citizenship Again

### Meta Ordered to Pay $942 Million in New Mexico Child-Safety Case
A New Mexico judge ordered Meta to create a $567 million fund addressing harm to children from its platforms, in addition to $375 million in civil penalties previously imposed in the case. The ruling followed findings that Meta misled users about the safety of Facebook and Instagram.

[The Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/tech/meta-ordered-to-pay-942-million-to-address-harm-to-kids-from-social-media-8ba5aab7?mod=rss_usnews) - [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/aug/06/new-mexico-court-meta)
