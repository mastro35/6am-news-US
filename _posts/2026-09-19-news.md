### U.S. and Denmark Reach Greenland Security Agreement

Trump said the United States and Denmark had reached an agreement concerning security in Greenland after months of threats to take control of the territory. The deal would allow the United States to expand its military presence, while Denmark and Greenland said it preserves their sovereignty and right to self-determination. The agreement’s full details were not immediately clear.

## National News

### Appeals Court Blocks Trump’s Third-Country Deportation Policy

A U.S. appeals court ruled that migrants must have an opportunity to raise safety concerns before being deported to countries other than their countries of origin. The decision restricts a Trump administration policy that had allowed rapid deportations to third countries. The case could reach the Supreme Court.

[The Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/immigration/2026/09/18/appeals-court-says-trump-administrations-third-country-deportation-policy-is-unlawful/) - [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/09/18/us/politics/appeals-court-trump-third-country-deportation.html)

### Texas Pauses Permits for Automated License-Plate Cameras

At Gov. Greg Abbott’s direction, the Texas Department of Transportation stopped issuing permits for Flock cameras and other automated license-plate readers along state roads. The pause does not affect nearly 900 cameras already installed along state rights-of-way, including those operated by the Texas Department of Public Safety.

[The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/sep/18/texas-flock-camera-permits)
