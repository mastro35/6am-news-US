### ICE shooting in New York fuels anger and concern in neighborhood
Residents of Manhattan’s Marble Hill neighborhood expressed anger and fear after an ICE agent shot Oscar Belgal, 28, while his 5-year-old child was in the car, according to reports. Belgal survived and was being held at a detention facility outside New York. Federal officials defended the agents involved. ### Judge rules Trump administration’s use of voter data unlawful
A federal judge ruled that the Justice Department’s policy of collecting unredacted state voter rolls and checking them against a federal immigration database was unlawful. The ruling is a setback for the administration’s effort to investigate noncitizen voting ahead of the midterm elections. [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/oct/09/federal-judge-rules-against-trump-push-state-voter-rolls) - [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/9/us-judge-rules-trump-administrations-use-of-voter-data-unlawful?traffic_source=rss)

### Livestreamed firing-squad execution plan draws bipartisan criticism
The Pentagon says the planned execution of Fort Hood shooter Nidal Hasan by firing squad will be livestreamed. Vice President JD Vance questioned whether the plan would go ahead and said he would not watch. Legal experts say the execution could face a challenge in federal court. [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/09/us/politics/fort-hood-execution-legal-challenges.html) - [BBC News](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ck20r39nl3qzo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

### Trump administration imposes sanctions on International Criminal Court
The Trump administration announced sanctions intended to cut the International Criminal Court off from the global financial system, with potential penalties for countries that do business with the court. The ICC condemned the move, and several member states, including the United Kingdom, said they strongly disagreed.
