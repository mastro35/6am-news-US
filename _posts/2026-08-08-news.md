### Senate confirms Todd Blanche as attorney general

The Senate confirmed Todd Blanche, a former personal attorney to President Donald Trump, as attorney general. Republican Sen. Bill Cassidy cast the deciding vote after Susan Collins and Lisa Murkowski opposed the nomination; every Democrat voted against it.

### Weak July jobs report pushes stocks to records

The U.S. economy lost 23,000 jobs in July, contrary to analysts’ expectations of job growth. The report prompted traders to reduce expectations for a Federal Reserve rate hike, while all three major stock indexes rose and the S&P 500 reached a record.

### 56 sets of remains found at Chicago funeral home

Authorities found the improperly stored and decomposing remains of 56 people at a Chicago funeral home. Illinois officials suspended the license of one of the directors, alleging that bodies were kept in an unrefrigerated area under “deplorable conditions.”

[The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/aug/07/chicago-funeral-home-bodies) - [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c86nv0elnzlo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

### California wildfire kills one as police arrest suspected spark source

At least one person died in the Gann fire in California’s Calaveras County. Authorities say the blaze may have been started by a man using a chainsaw; police arrested 65-year-old Herbert Wayne Smith. The fire has destroyed seven buildings and burned more than 15 square miles.

[The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/aug/07/sierra-nevada-foothills-fire-death)

### Puerto Rico begins water rationing amid severe drought

Puerto Rico began 48-hour water rationing for more than 180,000 households as severe drought depleted reservoirs. Some residents are expected to go without water for two days at a time throughout August.
